## Terms

- **Option**: one entry of the list sent with a query: a tool on MetaTool, an API on StableToolBench, a choice on MMLU.
- **Correct option**: an option that the dataset labels as correct for the query.
- **Distractor**: an option that is not a correct option.
- **NOTA**: the option "None" on MetaTool and "None of the above" on MMLU. It is the correct option of a list that holds no other correct option.
- **Paraphrased options**: 5 descriptions of one correct tool or API in 5 styles, written by Claude Opus 5.5. Each of the 5 is a correct option.
- **Duplicate options**: 5 copies of one correct option whose names differ only by extra spaces. Each of the 5 is a correct option.
- **Similar distractor**: a tool of the list of an example of the MetaTool test file `similar_tools` that is not the correct tool.
- **Random distractor**: an option of the dataset that is not in the list of the example, chosen with a seed.
- **Prediction**: the option that the model selects, the field `choice` of its answer.
- **Confidence**: the highest probability of an answer. It is not the field `confidence` of the model's answer.
- **Rejected list**: a list that the model's API does not answer, with status 413 or 422. Laya does this to a list that is too long for it. On 2026-10-08 it answered a list of 16 APIs with descriptions and rejected the same list with 20.
- **Refused list**: a list for which the model returns an answer of type `refusal` and no probabilities.
- **Accuracy**: the share of the answers whose prediction is a correct option.
- **Exact match**: for a query with k correct options, k being 2 or more: the share of the answers whose k options with the highest probabilities are the k correct options. A tie for place k is not a match.
- **Overconfidence**: mean confidence minus accuracy.
- **ECE (expected calibration error)**: the answers are put in 10 bins by confidence, from 0 to 0.1 up to 0.9 to 1.0. Each bin gives the distance between its mean confidence and its accuracy. ECE is the mean of the 10 distances, weighted by the number of answers in the bin.
- **Entropy**: the sum of -p log2(p) over the probabilities p of an answer, in bits. It is 0 when one option has 1.00, 2.00 for 4 options at 0.25 and 2.32 for 5 options at 0.20.
- **Highest correct minus lowest correct**: the highest probability of a correct option minus the lowest probability of a correct option.
- **Lowest correct minus highest distractor**: the lowest probability of a correct option minus the highest probability of a distractor. It is negative when a distractor has a higher probability than a correct option.
- **Highest correct minus highest distractor**: the highest probability of a correct option minus the highest probability of a distractor. For each answer it is the sum of the two differences above. Two correct options at 0.50 each give 0.00, +0.50 and +0.50 for the three differences.
- **Prediction minus runner-up**: the highest probability of an answer minus its second-highest probability.
- **Position**: the place of an option in the list sent, counted from the first.

## Notes

- Every figure of a row is computed over the lists that the model answered. A rejected or refused list has no answer.
- Laya gets each list in a request of its own. Jev, Liquid d1 and GPT-6 Luna Decisions get the lists of one example in one request.
- The row with one list in the written order exists for Jev only. The code that built its lists is not the current code.
- Where a model rejected or refused lists of a row with cyclic permutations, the options are no longer at each position equally often in that row.
- The three differences and the probabilities are computed for each answer and then averaged over the answers.
- In 3 StableToolBench queries (`G1_category/18337`, `G1_category/21447`, `G1_category/77247`) all 5 APIs of the list are correct. Where a cell gives a number of answers in brackets, the figure leaves out the answers to these queries.
- MetaTool `similar_tools` and `scenario`, the tool list of the example and "None": each example has 6 lists, the released order and the correct tool at the first place, a quarter, the middle, three quarters and the last place. "None" is the last option. `reliability` has the released order only.
- MetaTool `multi_tool`: each example has 9 lists, the released order, the two correct tools next to each other at 5 places, and apart at 3 pairs of places.
- MetaTool list length: each example has 5 lists per length, with the correct tool at the 5 places above. "None" is the last option.
- The rows with 5 paraphrased options, 5 duplicate options, distractors and NOTA, 5 random distractors and the 4 choices of an MMLU question use every cyclic permutation of one list. Each option is at each position once per example. Two rows with paraphrased options have one list per example and say so.
- In four rows the mean probability at a position also depends on which option the lists have there: the MetaTool row with 5 tools, where position 6 is "None"; the row with 1 correct API, which uses the released order; and the two rows with one list per example.
