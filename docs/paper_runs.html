Runs on every example: figures by run and model

Runs of docs/paper_plan.md, made on 2026-10-11 on Jev, Liquid d1 and GPT-6 Luna Decisions. Each run has one table with the models as rows. python scripts/runs_report.py paper_runs --out docs/paper_runs.html rebuilds this page from the results folders and src/grjev/definitions/paper_runs.json.

Terms

  • Option: one entry of the list sent with a query: a tool on MetaTool, a choice on MMLU, a passage on HotpotQA, an article on ECtHR.
  • Correct option: an option that the dataset labels as correct for the query.
  • Distractor: an option that is not a correct option.
  • NOTA: an option that says no other option is correct: "None" on MetaTool, "None of the above" on MMLU, "None of the passages" on HotpotQA, "None of these articles" on ECtHR. It is the correct option of a list that holds no other correct option.
  • Prediction: the option that the model selects, the field choice of its answer.
  • Confidence: the highest probability of an answer. It is not the field confidence of the model's answer.
  • Refused list: a list for which the model returns an answer of type refusal and no probabilities.
  • Accuracy: the share of the answers whose prediction is a correct option.
  • Exact match: for a list with k correct options, k being 2 or more: the share of the answers whose k options with the highest probabilities are the k correct options. A tie for place k is not a match.
  • Overconfidence: mean confidence minus accuracy.
  • ECE (expected calibration error): the answers are put in 10 bins by confidence, from 0 to 0.1 up to 0.9 to 1.0. Each bin gives the distance between its mean confidence and its accuracy. ECE is the mean of the 10 distances, weighted by the number of answers in the bin.
  • Prediction minus runner-up: the highest probability of an answer minus its second-highest probability.
  • Highest correct minus lowest correct: the highest probability of a correct option minus the lowest probability of a correct option.
  • Highest correct minus highest distractor: the highest probability of a correct option minus the highest probability of a distractor.
  • Lowest correct minus highest distractor: the lowest probability of a correct option minus the highest probability of a distractor. It is negative when a distractor has a higher probability than a correct option.
  • Entropy: the sum of -p log2(p) over the probabilities p of an answer, in bits. It is 0 when one option has 1.00. Its maximum is log2 of the number of options, reached when every option has the same probability, and the header of the column gives it.
  • Position: the place of an option in the list sent, counted from the first.

Notes

  • Jev is jev-1.13.0, asked through TypeSafe's API. Liquid d1 (liquid/d1) and GPT-6 Luna Decisions (openai/gpt-6-luna-decisions) are asked through Vercel AI Gateway, which takes no model version. The three models get the same requests.
  • Every figure of a row is computed over the lists that the model answered. A refused list has no answer. Jev and Liquid d1 refused no list.
  • A column is left out of a table when no model has that figure: a list with no correct option has no accuracy, and a list with only correct options has no distractor.
  • The three differences and the probabilities are computed for each answer and then averaged over the answers. The differences with a distractor use the answers to lists that hold a correct option and a distractor. Where a list of a run holds only correct options, the column "Lists with a correct option and a distractor" gives their number.
  • The MMLU runs use a seeded sample of 1,000 of the 13,096 questions whose choices can be moved. The HotpotQA runs with 5 cyclic permutations and the HotpotQA run with 4 distractors and NOTA use a seeded sample of 1,000 of the 7,345 questions with 10 passages. The other runs use every example.
  • MetaTool repeats queries across its test files. The 1,767 examples of the run with paraphrases give 1,047 different requests, and the 3,292 examples of the run with duplicates 3,275. An answer to a repeated request is counted once per example.
  • The labels of ECtHR are the articles that the Court found violated in a case, and a case with no label is one in which it found no violation of the 10 articles.
  • The yes/no question of a HotpotQA passage is sent for 500 questions, and that of an ECtHR article for the 1,000 test cases.
