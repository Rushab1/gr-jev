\section{Lists with no correct choice}
\label{app:abstention}
We construct lists with no correct choice in two ways. We remove the correct choices from an example's list. We also sample choices from the dataset that are absent from the example's list. We send lists without a ``none of the above'' choice and lists with one in separate runs. Table~\ref{tab:runs} gives the number of lists of each kind. Without it, we report the confidence of the prediction. With it, ``none of the above'' is the only correct choice, and we report the share of predictions that select it.

Without a ``none of the above'' choice, the mean confidence is 0.49 to 0.87, and it is lowest on HotpotQA for each model (Table~\ref{tab:abstention}). With it, the models select ``none of the above'' in 93\% to 100\% of the lists of random choices. On lists of the example's own distractors, they select it in 79\% to 88\% of the HotpotQA lists and in 7\% to 37\% of the ECtHR lists (Table~\ref{tab:nota}).
