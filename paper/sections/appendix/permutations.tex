\section{Permutations of the lists}
\label{app:permutations}
We send each MetaTool list with two correct tools in 10 permutations. In five permutations the two correct tools are next to each other, at places 1 and 2, 3 and 4, 5 and 6, 7 and 8, and 9 and 10. In the other five they are 5 places apart, at places 1 and 6, 2 and 7, 3 and 8, 4 and 9, and 5 and 10. The other tools keep their released order. Places 7 and 8 give the released order. For MetaTool, each column of Table~\ref{tab:allocation} is the mean over the 10 permutations. We send each ECtHR list in its 10 cyclic permutations. For ECtHR, each column of Table~\ref{tab:allocation} is the mean over the 10 permutations. We send each HotpotQA list in all of its cyclic permutations. Each passage appears once at each position. 7,345 of the 7,405 lists have 10 passages and 10 permutations. The other 60 have 2 to 9 passages and as many permutations. The first permutation is the released order. For HotpotQA, each column of Table~\ref{tab:allocation} is the mean over all permutations.
