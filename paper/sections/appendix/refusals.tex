\section{Refusals of GPT-6 Luna Decisions}
\label{app:refusals}
GPT-6 Luna Decisions refused 20,404 of 186,103 questions (11.0\%): 14,411 of 21,460 lists of random choices, 3,290 of 14,231 lists made of the example's distractors, 2,054 of 34,530 lists with only correct choices, 511 of the 90,912 other lists, and 138 of 24,970 yes/no questions. A refusal has no probabilities and no reason. We sent one refused request again seven times, with one change each time. The model answered the same passages when the query was changed to one that a passage fits, and the same query when a ``none of the above'' choice or a fitting passage was added.
