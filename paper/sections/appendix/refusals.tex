\section{Refusals of GPT-6 Luna Decisions}
\label{app:refusals}
GPT-6 Luna Decisions refuses 19,820 of the 259,395 lists and yes/no questions in its 43,022 requests (7.6\%). It refuses 14,420 of 31,435 lists of random choices, 1,374 of 25,695 lists of identical copies, 3,888 of 164,892 other lists, 138 of 24,970 yes/no questions, and none of the 12,403 ChaosNLI lists. A refusal returns no probabilities and no reason. We sent one refused request again seven times, with one change each time. The model answered when we kept the passages and replaced the query with one that a passage is relevant to. It also answered when we kept the query and added a ``none of the above'' choice or a relevant passage.
