For a yes/no question, a probability of yes below 0.5 is an answer of no. The thresholds that maximize the exact set accuracy of the yes/no questions are below 0.5 for 6 of the 9 models and datasets, and 0.13 or lower on HotpotQA. At 0.5, the exact set accuracy of the yes/no questions is 0.07 to 0.49 (Figure~\ref{fig:exact-set}).
