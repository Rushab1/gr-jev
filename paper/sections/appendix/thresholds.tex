\section{Selecting by a threshold}
\label{app:thresholds}
A probability of yes below 0.5 counts as an answer of no. The threshold with the highest exact set accuracy on the yes/no questions is below 0.5 for 6 of the 9 pairs of model and dataset (Table~\ref{tab:threshold-detail}). At a threshold of 0.5, the exact set accuracy of the yes/no questions is 0.07 to 0.49 (Figure~\ref{fig:exact-set}).
