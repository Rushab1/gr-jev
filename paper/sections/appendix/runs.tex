\section{Runs}
\label{app:runs}
Each model receives 43,022 requests. Lists of identical copies account for 5,139 requests. Yes/no questions account for 497 MetaTool requests, 1,000 HotpotQA requests, and 1,000 ECtHR requests. ChaosNLI lists account for 4,645 requests. HotpotQA lists in their cyclic permutations account for 7,423 requests. Table~\ref{tab:runs} gives the number of examples and lists of each kind.
