\section{Position of the correct tool}
\label{app:position}
% The numbers are from results/metatool_position*/2026-10-11_01/rows.jsonl, lists first and last of the test files
% similar_tools and scenario. The change is computed before rounding.
For 2,795 MetaTool queries with one correct tool, from the test files \texttt{similar\_tools} and \texttt{scenario}, we placed the correct tool first or tenth in a list of 10 tools that ends with the choice ``None''. The other nine tools keep their order. GPT-6 Luna Decisions refused 2 of the lists.
