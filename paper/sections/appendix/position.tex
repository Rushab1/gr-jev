\section{Position of the correct tool}
\label{app:position}
% The numbers are from results/metatool_position*/2026-10-11_01/rows.jsonl, lists first and last of the test files
% similar_tools and scenario. The change is computed before rounding.
For 2,795 MetaTool queries with one correct tool in the \texttt{similar\_tools} and \texttt{scenario} test files, we place the correct tool first or last among 5, 10, or 15 tools. We add the choice ``None'' after the tools. The other tools keep their order. For 2 of the 2,795 queries, GPT-6 Luna Decisions refuses every list.
