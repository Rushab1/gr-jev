\section*{Limitations}
We study three decision models, Jev, Liquid d1 and GPT-6 Luna Decisions, through their hosted APIs. Generative language models that select a choice in text are outside the scope of this study. We use four English datasets, MetaTool, HotpotQA, ECtHR and ChaosNLI, and take the labels of the first three as the set of correct choices. We study selection by one threshold for all examples of a dataset and by one yes/no question per choice. Other tasks with several correct choices, such as the diagnoses supported by a medical record or the vulnerability types present in code, and other selection rules, such as a threshold per example or a fixed number of selected choices, are left to future work.
