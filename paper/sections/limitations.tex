\section*{Limitations}
We sent each request once and report no confidence intervals. Vercel AI Gateway does not give a version for Liquid d1 or GPT-6 Luna Decisions. Our measures depend on the labels of the datasets. HotpotQA labels a passage as correct when it contains a supporting sentence, ECtHR labels the articles that the Court found violated rather than every article that the facts concern, and the distractors of a MetaTool query can include a tool that fits it. The identical copies differ in their random IDs, which the APIs require. Jev and GPT-6 Luna Decisions return probabilities with two decimals, so a probability below 0.005 appears as 0. Some HotpotQA runs use a sample of 1,000 questions, and the figures of GPT-6 Luna Decisions cover only the lists it answered. Claude Opus 5.5 wrote the 24 support tickets, and no second reader checked them.
